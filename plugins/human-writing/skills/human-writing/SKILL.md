# Human writing

## Core principle

Build around concrete meaning, natural emphasis, and the user's purpose. Diagnose patterns and clusters, not isolated watched words. Treat a supplied writing sample as the controlling voice reference.

## Select the mode

Select `draft` or `rewrite`, then select `general prose` or `fiction`. Read `references/surface-prose.md` for every task. Read `references/fiction-craft.md` only for fiction or narrative prose. Read `references/sources.md` only when evidence, attribution, or limitations matter.

## Preserve the content contract

For non-fiction drafting, use only supplied facts and do not invent factual details, claims, sources, commitments, names, dates, numbers, quotations, or attributions. For any rewrite, preserve concrete facts, required functional meaning, story facts, and voice; do not invent those factual categories. In promotional rewrites only, treat unsupported evaluative self-description as presentation rather than fact. Outside promotional copy, preserve supplied personal meaning unless asked to alter it. When drafting fiction, invent only within the user's stated premise and requested scope. Preserve terminology, deliberate choices, and defensible voice quirks unless the user asks otherwise. Do not claim human authorship, promise detector evasion, or imitate a named living author.

## Compose and revise

Lead with the real point. Use supplied detail instead of invented specificity. Shape rhythm and structure around emphasis. For fiction, apply research as a story-specific diagnostic, never as a universal rule. Run the audit internally: check meaning, voice, evidence, proportion, cadence, and accidental generic phrasing.

Match the requested length before finalising. When the user gives a word range, count the draft and revise it into that range; aim near the middle unless the requested form needs a different balance.

For a constrained announcement, state the decision, supplied reason, and practical change plainly. Give only the context needed to make the update useful, then stop; do not turn a narrow decision into a broader promise. For a promotional rewrite, separate supported functional propositions from evaluative self-description. Preserve what the product combines, supports, or enables. Unsupported claims about importance or novelty, and category-level or future framing, are tone rather than factual claims. Contrastive self-positioning is tone too. Preserve such evaluation only when the source supplies evidence. Use direct declarative sentences in modest everyday language. Render the supported function and stop.

## Output contract

Return exactly the final prose unless the user explicitly requests analysis, alternatives or options, rationale or explanation, tracked changes, an audit, or another output shape. When requested, provide only that requested shape. Omit acknowledgements, process narration, and offers to do more.

## Common mistakes

Do not replace one conspicuous word while leaving the surrounding pattern intact. Do not polish away intentional plainness, uncertainty, or idiosyncrasy. Do not add a conclusion that repeats rather than completes the text.
