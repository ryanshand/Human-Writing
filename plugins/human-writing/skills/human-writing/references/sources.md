# Sources and boundaries

## Humanizer

Surface guidance was synthesized from the `humanizer` skill, version 2.9.1. It is MIT licensed and derived from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" guide.

## StoryScope

Credit Jenna Russell, Rishanth Rajendhran, Chau Minh Pham, Mohit Iyyer, and John Wieting: [repository](https://github.com/jenna-russell/storyscope) and [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03136). Repository code is under MIT.

## Research boundary

Interpret the paper within its study: 10,272 prompts; five LLM sources plus human stories; approximately 5,000 words per story; and 304 features across 10 dimensions. Narrative features achieved 93.2% macro-F1 in the paper's held-out binary task, while the 30-feature core achieved 84.8%.

## Interpretation boundary

Treat group-level findings as overlapping signals, not proof of the provenance of one text. Use them to improve deliberate narrative construction, not to certify authorship.

## Copyright boundary

Do not reconstruct or redistribute human story text: it is excluded from the released repository.
