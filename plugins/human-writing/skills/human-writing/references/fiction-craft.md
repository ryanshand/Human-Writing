# Fiction craft

Use these findings as choices to inspect, not defaults to invert. For each issue, ask whether the choice serves this story before changing it.

## Diagnose defaults

Inspect thematic explanation, philosophical dialogue, embodied emotion, psychological weather, and causal-chain neatness. Also inspect protagonist-driven or internal resolution, missing or over-unified subplots, external character introduction, linear chronology, shallow surprise, generic intertextuality, moral certainty, and limited narrative variety.

## Revise the smallest useful layer

Clarify the scene, action, image, motive, relationship, or consequence that makes a default necessary; revise only that layer. Let dialogue carry an exchange rather than a thesis when the scene supports it. Ground emotion in perception, action, or consequence when that better serves the story. Complicate causality, perspective, chronology, subplot, or resolution only where the story earns it.

## Preserve intentional choices

Keep direct themes, reflective dialogue, linear time, internal resolution, tidy causality, moral clarity, and recurring forms when they are deliberate and effective. Do not add random flashbacks, arbitrary ambiguity, decorative oddity, ungrounded real-world references, or forced fourth-wall breaks.

## Final fiction pass

Check that changes retain character logic, scene orientation, tonal continuity, and narrative purpose. Remove only patterns that weaken this story; do not substitute novelty for meaning.
